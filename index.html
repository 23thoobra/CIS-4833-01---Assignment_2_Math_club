About Us

The Maverick Math Club is a student organization open to every student on campus, not just math majors. We believe math is fun when you explore it with friends. Whether you love proofs, puzzles, or programming, there is a seat for you.

We meet every Thursday at 6:00 PM in Wissink Hall — snacks & whiteboards provided.

What We Do

  • Weekly problem-solving sessions
  • Competition prep
    • Putnam Mathematical Competition
    • Regional math modeling contest
    • Integration Bee
  • Peer tutoring for Calculus I & II
  • Guest speaker nights with faculty and alumni
  • Pi Day (3/14) celebration with a pie-eating contest

How to Join

  1. Visit our Events page and pick a meeting.
  2. Fill out the registration form on that page.
  3. Attend your first meeting and introduce yourself.
  4. Pay the semester dues (optional for General Members).
